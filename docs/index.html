🧗 HarnessSync

Your session, remembered.

A free climbing log for boulderers and rope climbers. Log a climb in a few taps, keep projects in view, and see how you’re progressing.

Log your first climb

Works in your phone browser. Sign in with Google to keep your log private and available next session.

What it does

  • Log climbs quickly by grade and send status, with optional route details.
  • Track projects across sessions: count attempts, keep beta notes, and graduate them when you send.
  • See your progress with a send volume pyramid and a grade progression chart.
  • Time your sessions at the gym or crag.
  • Compare with friends on an opt-in leaderboard.

Signing in

HarnessSync uses Google sign-in so your climbing log is private to you. Your climbs and projects are visible only to you. Leaderboard sharing is optional and off by default. We only receive your name and email address from Google, never your password. See the privacy policy for exactly what's stored and how to delete it.
