🧗 HarnessSync

A free climbing log for boulderers and rope climbers.

Open HarnessSync

What it does

  • Log climbs with grade (V-scale or YDS), send style, wall angle, hold type and location.
  • Track projects across sessions: count attempts, keep beta notes, and graduate them when you send.
  • See your progress with a send volume pyramid and a grade progression chart.
  • Time your sessions at the gym or crag.
  • Compare with friends on an opt-in leaderboard.

Signing in

HarnessSync uses Google sign-in so your climbing log is private to you. We only receive your name and email address from Google, never your password. See the privacy policy for exactly what's stored and how to delete it.
