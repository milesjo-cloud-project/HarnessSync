For climbing gyms

Give your climbers a reason to log

Put one flyer at the desk. Its QR code opens HarnessSync on the Log screen with your gym's name on it. Each month you get a one-page report on how your walls are being climbed.

No setup

No route database to keep up. Climbers log the grade and the try; you get the totals.

Opt-in only

Scanning the flyer never signs anyone up. Climbers tap to be counted, and can stop any time.

Small groups stay hidden

With fewer than three climbers in a month, the report holds back its numbers, so no one stands out.

At the gym. Out at the crag.

Your log goes where you climb

The app works in your phone browser, with nothing to install. For days without a signal, connect the separate phone logger while you're online and wait for it to confirm the download.

It saves climbs and tries on your device. Keep the logger open, or reopen it once you're back online, and it syncs.

  • A timer for your sessionsTrack time at the gym or crag.
  • Six languagesEnglish, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
  • Help when something goes wrongPrivate problem reports, status updates and owner replies.
