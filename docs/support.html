Help & support

Something isn't working? Here's how to get help.

Can you open the app?

Use Help & report a problem in the app to send a private report. Tell us what you clicked, what happened, and what you expected. Include an incident reference if the app showed one. You can follow the status and read the owner's reply in My reports.

Open HarnessSync

Can't sign in?

Check that your browser allows sign-in cookies and try signing in with Google again. If the app is sleeping, use the wake-up control and wait for it to load. If a save failed, check your history before trying again to avoid duplicate records.

Can't use the app at all?

You can report a general app failure through GitHub. This requires a GitHub account. GitHub reports are public. Include only the affected feature, browser/device type, steps to reproduce, and incident reference. Never include your email, climbing history, passwords, verification codes, or screenshots containing private information.

Report a public app issue

What happens next?

The owner reviews reports and investigates failures. In-app reports can move through Received, Investigating, In progress, Fixed, or Closed. A report is marked Fixed after the owner verifies and deploys the change. This hobby project does not promise an immediate response.

Optional support assistant

When enabled, the assistant can explain the app and help investigate reports you allow it to process. It cannot access your climbing log, change account data, or deploy fixes. Its suggestions may be mistaken. Reports work even when the assistant is unavailable.
