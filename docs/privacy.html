Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

HarnessSync ("the app") is a free climbing log run as an independent hobby project. This page explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your email address and name. The email identifies your account. Your name is only used to suggest a display name the first time you sign in. We never see your Google password.
  • Your display name and whether you want to appear on the leaderboard.
  • What you log: climbs (grade, send style, date, route name, location, wall angle, hold type), projects and their notes, and session start/end times.
  • Support reports: your message, affected area, optional reproduction steps, expected behavior, incident reference, rating, and the owner's status and reply. Legacy AI permissions from the retired assistant may remain in older records but are no longer used.
  • Crash diagnostics: error type, code locations, affected feature, timestamps, and occurrence counts. We exclude exception messages, local variables and account identifiers.
  • Phone app waitlist: if you join it, which phone you use and any answers you give about what you'd want in a phone app.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The app sets one sign-in cookie to keep you signed in.

How it's used

  • To show you your own climb history, projects, charts and sessions.
  • To show your display name, best grade and send count on the public leaderboard, only if you opt in. Your email is never shown to other users.
  • Support reports are private to you and the owner. Notifications may be emailed to the owner with your account email; status updates and replies appear in My reports. Failed notifications are queued for retry, and delivered notification bodies are cleared from that queue.
  • The app does not run an AI chatbot or automatically send reports to a model. To investigate a problem, the owner may manually share reviewed, redacted reproduction details with Codex Cloud. Repair exports omit account identifiers and climbing history and redact common email/token formats. The owner reviews for remaining private details before sharing.
  • If you join the phone app waitlist, your answers help decide whether to build the app, and we'll email you about it. You can leave the waitlist at any time from the 📱 Phone App tab.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Where it's stored

The app relies on these services to run:

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Streamlit Community Cloud hosts the app.
  • Neon stores the database (hosted in the United States).
  • Gmail delivers feedback messages to the developer.
  • Google Forms collects answers to the phone app survey linked from our home page.
  • OpenAI Codex Cloud may process reviewed reproduction details manually shared by the owner to prepare code fixes. It has no automatic connection to the support database. Processing follows the owner's applicable OpenAI account settings and terms.
  • GitHub hosts public reports submitted through the fallback support link. GitHub requires its own account; these reports are public and separate from your app account. When the owner enables scheduled email retries, GitHub Actions also runs the delivery worker, which temporarily reads queued notification contents from the database to email the owner. The worker logs counts and generic errors, not report contents or account addresses.

Phone app survey

The survey on our home page is a Google Form, separate from the app. It asks how you climb, which phone you use and which features you'd want, plus your email address if you choose to give it so we can tell you when the app is ready. Answers are stored in Google Forms, used only to decide whether and how to build a phone app, and never sold or shared. To have your answers deleted, ask through Help & report a problem in the app. Google's handling of the form is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Leaderboard: hide yourself at any time from the 👤 account menu in the app's sidebar.
  • Export: download your climbs and session history as CSV files from the Dashboard, and your active projects as a CSV file from the Projects tab.
  • Delete: use "Delete my account & data" in the 👤 account menu. It permanently removes your profile, climbs, projects, sessions, support reports, legacy assistant usage, queued notifications linked to your account, and waitlist signup right away. Anonymous crash counts remain. Copies already delivered to the owner's email and public GitHub reports are separate; request removal of those copies through support.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Help & report a problem in the app. If you cannot use the app, see support options.
